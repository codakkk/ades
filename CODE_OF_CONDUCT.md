# Code of conduct

`ades` welcomes people who want to improve the library, test it against real signatures and smart cards, or review its security. Take part with curiosity, patience and respect.

Expected behaviour includes giving actionable technical feedback, assuming good faith while still challenging claims with evidence, respecting privacy, and making room for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualised attention, doxxing and deliberate misrepresentation are not acceptable. Neither is publishing anyone's personal data: that includes real signatures, certificates, names and codice fiscale, even in a bug report. Do not use project spaces to promote forged signatures, credential theft or other abuse.

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation or ban an account when needed to protect contributors and users. Report sensitive conduct concerns privately, through the repository's **Security** tab (**Report a vulnerability**), and mark the report as a code-of-conduct matter.
